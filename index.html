How it works

No API, no server, no hidden magic — the whole model lives in your browser tab.

1. Learn

The text is split into overlapping word sequences of length N (the order). For each sequence, the model remembers which words followed it in training.

2. Generate

Starting from a random sequence (or your seed), it repeatedly samples a likely next word and slides the window forward — like predictive text with amnesia.

3. Tune

Higher order = more faithful to the source. Lower temperature = safer, more common words; higher = wilder jumps. Your edits retrain instantly and persist offline.

Honest limits: Markov chains remix patterns from the training text — they do not understand meaning. Output quality grows with the amount of text you feed it. This is a creative toy and teaching tool, not a large language model.
